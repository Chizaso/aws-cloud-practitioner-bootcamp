1. Regions these are physical locations in the world where AWS has its data centers
2. An availability zone is one or more discrete data ccenters within a Region, each with independent power, cooling and networking.
3. AWS uses multilple AZs because if one AZ fails, the workload can automatically fail over to another AZ making it high availablity. Its also reduces the risk of downtime due to hardwareor power failures
4. basic ideal of AWS global Infrastructure - The regions which across the globe to bring services closer to users, multiple AZS per region for resilience and redundancy.
